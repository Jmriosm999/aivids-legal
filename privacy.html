Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AIVIDS may handle information when users use the application and connect supported services.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, AIVIDS may process account information, authentication information, video content, metadata, and information required to operate connected social media integrations.

3. Connected Social Media Accounts

When a user connects a supported social media account, AIVIDS may receive information and authorization tokens permitted by the applicable platform and the permissions granted by the user.

4. Use of Information

Information may be used to authenticate users, operate requested features, manage connected accounts, create and publish content, maintain the service, and improve reliability and security.

5. Data Security

AIVIDS is designed to use reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by the service. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Sharing

AIVIDS does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to third-party services when necessary to provide a feature requested by the user, such as publishing content to a connected social media platform.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, maintain security, comply with applicable requirements, or resolve disputes.

8. User Control

Users may disconnect supported social media accounts and stop using AIVIDS at any time, subject to the functionality and policies of the connected platforms.

9. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service or applicable requirements change.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact information provided by AIVIDS.
